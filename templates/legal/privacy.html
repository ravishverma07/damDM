Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data {{ BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME }} ("{{ APP_NAME }}", "we", "us") collects when you use {{ APP_BASE_URL }} and the {{ APP_NAME }} service, why, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws.

What we collect

About you, the account holder

  • Account details: your email address, name and, if you use a password, a securely hashed version of it (never the password itself).
  • Sign-in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture, as shared by Google.
  • Instagram connection: your Instagram professional account ID, username, profile picture, basic details of your posts, and an access token that we store encrypted and use only to call Meta's official API. We never ask for or store your Instagram password.
  • Your automations: the keywords, replies, messages and links you set up.
  • Billing: your plan, subscription status and payment history (amount, date, method type such as UPI or card, and Razorpay's reference numbers). Razorpay handles your payment details; we never see or store your full card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Trial and referral checks: to stop the free trial or referral rewards being claimed more than once, we keep one-way hashes (fingerprints) of your email, Google and Instagram account IDs, browser ID, and the UPI ID or saved card behind your AutoPay. A hash can't be turned back into the original value.
  • Referral payouts: the UPI ID you give us for withdrawals, stored encrypted.
  • Technical data: a hashed (not raw) IP address, your browser type, timezone, and records of sign-ins and important account actions, for security.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you contact support.

About people who interact with your Instagram account

To run your automations, {{ APP_NAME }} receives from Meta the comments, messages, story replies and mentions that people send to your account, along with their Instagram username, profile picture and Instagram-scoped ID. When they tap a link we sent, we record the click with a hashed IP address and browser type. We process this data on your behalf and on your instructions, only to provide the service to you. If you use {{ APP_NAME }}, you are responsible for telling your audience how you use their data where the law requires it.

How we use it

  • to provide the service: sign you in, connect Instagram, run automations and show you results;
  • to take payments, manage your plan and trial, and pay referral rewards;
  • to prevent fraud and abuse, including repeat trials and self-referrals;
  • to keep the service secure and fix problems;
  • to send you messages about your account, billing and important changes; and
  • to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your data because you have agreed to it when signing up, because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, or because the law requires it. We don't sell your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run {{ APP_NAME }}, and only as much as they need:

  • Meta (Instagram), to connect your account and send and receive messages;
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google;
  • Razorpay, to process payments and AutoPay;
  • Cloudflare and our hosting providers, to deliver and protect the website; and
  • authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our users or the service from fraud or harm.

Some of these providers may process data outside India, with appropriate safeguards.

Cookies and similar technology

We use only the cookies needed to run the site:

  • Sign-in and security (sessionid, csrftoken): keep you signed in and protect forms.
  • Browser ID (damdm_device): a random ID used to prevent free-trial and referral abuse.
  • Referral (damdm_ref): remembers an invite link for 30 days.
  • Timezone (damdm_tz): shows dates in your local time.
  • Theme: your light or dark mode choice, saved in your browser.

Google (for sign-in) and Razorpay (at checkout) may set their own cookies under their own policies. We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your data to provide the service.
  • Disconnecting Instagram, in {{ APP_NAME }} or by removing {{ APP_NAME }} in Instagram's settings, deletes the stored access token immediately and pauses that account's automations.
  • A data deletion request made through Instagram deletes that Instagram account's connection, posts, contacts, comments and messages from {{ APP_NAME }}, usually within an hour. You get a confirmation code to check its progress.
  • Deleting your account in {{ APP_NAME }} closes it and signs you out; your records are kept. To have your personal data erased, email us (see below) and we will do so within 30 days.
  • Payment and tax records are kept for as long as Indian law requires, usually up to 8 years, even after erasure.
  • Trial and referral fingerprints (hashes only) are kept after an account is deleted, so the same person can't claim a new free trial or referral reward.

How we protect it

We use HTTPS for all traffic, encrypt Instagram tokens and UPI IDs at rest, store passwords and fingerprints only as hashes, and limit who can access personal data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it (you can change most details yourself in Settings);
  • erase it, subject to the records we must keep by law;
  • withdraw your consent, for example by disconnecting Instagram or closing your account; and
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.

Email {{ PRIVACY_EMAIL }} from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days. See also data deletion instructions.

Children

{{ APP_NAME }} is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
