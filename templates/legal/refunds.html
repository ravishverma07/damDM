Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

{{ APP_NAME }} is a subscription service provided by {{ BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME }}. The Pro plan costs ₹99 per month (inclusive of applicable taxes) and is billed through Razorpay using UPI AutoPay, cards or netbanking. {{ APP_NAME }} is a digital service; nothing is shipped.

Free trial

  • New customers get a 14-day free trial. You approve an AutoPay mandate when you start, but the first ₹99 is only charged when the trial ends.
  • Your bank or Razorpay may take a small verification amount to set up the mandate. It is refunded automatically.
  • If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time from Plan & billing in your account. Cancelling stops all future charges.
  • After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the trial or the month you have already paid for.
  • You can also revoke the AutoPay mandate from your UPI app or bank. This stops future charges in the same way.

Refunds

  • Monthly payments are generally non-refundable once the month has started, because the service is available to you straight away.
  • We refund in full if you were charged twice for the same month, charged after you cancelled, or charged because of an error on our side.
  • If the service was unavailable for a long period because of a fault on our side, write to us and we will review a full or partial refund.
  • To request a refund, email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} within 7 days of the charge, with your account email and the payment date.

How refunds are paid

We reply to refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) and usually arrive within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Questions

See our contact page or email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.
