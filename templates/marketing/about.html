Why we built {{ APP_NAME }}

Instagram is where many Indian creators, coaches and small businesses find their customers. But the people who comment “price?” or “link please” expect an answer quickly, and replying to each of them by hand takes hours. The tools that automate this were often priced in US dollars, charged more as an audience grew, and took a long time to learn.

We wanted something simpler: connect Instagram, pick a template, and go live in a minute, for one small monthly price with everything included.
