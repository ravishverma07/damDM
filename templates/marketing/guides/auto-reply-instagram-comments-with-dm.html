Why comment-to-DM works

Links in captions aren't clickable, and asking people to visit your bio loses most of them along the way. Asking for a one-word comment is easy, and it helps you in two ways:

  • More reach. Comments show Instagram that people are interested in your post, which can help it reach more people.
  • More clicks. The link lands in the person's DMs, where they can tap it straight away or find it later.

Doing this by hand works for ten comments, not for five hundred. That's where automation comes in: a tool like {{ APP_NAME }} replies to every matching comment within seconds.

What you need

  • An Instagram Business or Creator account. See how to switch.
  • Allow access to messages turned on in Instagram (Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools).
  • A tool that uses Meta's official Instagram API. Never give your password to a tool.

Set it up step by step

  1. Connect Instagram. Sign up for {{ APP_NAME }} and connect your account through Instagram's login page.
  2. Pick the template. Go to Automations → New and choose Send a link when people comment.
  3. Choose the posts. Run it on one Reel, all your posts, or your next post.
  4. Add your keyword. For example LINK. Add more if people might type something else, like LINKS or SEND.
  5. Turn on public replies. Add two or three short replies such as “Sent! Check your DMs 💌”. They rotate, so your comments look natural.
  6. Write the DM. Thank them, say what the link is, and add a button with your URL.
  7. Turn it on and test. Ask a friend to comment the keyword from their account.

Want followers too? Switch on follow to unlock, so people follow you before the link is sent.

Choosing a keyword

  • Short and obvious: LINK, GUIDE, PRICE, RECIPE.
  • Matched to the offer: “Comment RECIPE” is clearer than “Comment YES”.
  • Easy to type on a phone: avoid long or unusual words.
  • One per post: if you run several automations, give each post its own keyword.

Captions that get comments

Say the keyword clearly, early, and more than once: on screen in the Reel and in the caption.

  • “Comment GUIDE and I'll DM you the free checklist.”
  • “Want the full recipe? Comment RECIPE 👇”
  • “Comment PRICE for today's offer, sent straight to your inbox.”

Mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting to turn on message access in Instagram's settings.
  • A DM with no clear next step. Always include one button.
  • Sending something different from what you promised. It breaks trust quickly.
  • Waiting days to set it up. Instagram only allows a private reply within 7 days of a comment, so have the automation ready before you post.
