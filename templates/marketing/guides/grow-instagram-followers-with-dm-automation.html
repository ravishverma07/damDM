The idea in one line

Offer something useful, ask people to comment a keyword to get it, and send it only after they follow you. The comment helps your post reach more people; the follow keeps them around.

Create an offer worth following for

The offer should be quick to use and clearly linked to what you post about. Good examples:

  • A one-page checklist, template or cheat sheet
  • Presets, wallpapers or design files
  • A recipe card, workout plan or study notes
  • A discount code or early access to a launch

Ask yourself: would someone who wants this also enjoy your future posts? If yes, you'll gain followers who stay.

Set up follow to unlock

  1. In {{ APP_NAME }}, create an automation from the Grow followers before sharing template.
  2. Set the keyword, for example GUIDE, and choose the post.
  3. Write a friendly follow request: “Almost there! Follow me and tap the button, and I'll send it.”
  4. Add the DM people get once they follow, with a button to your offer.
  5. Turn it on. {{ APP_NAME }} checks the follow before sending the link, and lets people try again if they haven't followed yet.

Post ideas that work

  • “Save this” Reels with a longer version available by DM.
  • Before and after results, with the method as the freebie.
  • Mistakes lists (“5 mistakes I made…”), with the full checklist by DM.
  • Story polls followed by “Reply YES for the link”, using story reply automation.

What to measure

  • Comments per post compared with your usual posts
  • New followers in the days after the post
  • Link clicks from the DM
  • Unfollows over the next few weeks, to check the offer attracts the right people

What to avoid

  • Bait-and-switch offers. Deliver exactly what you promised.
  • Offers unrelated to your content. They bring followers who leave.
  • Unwanted messages. Only message people who asked; Instagram's rules require it, and {{ APP_NAME }} follows them.
