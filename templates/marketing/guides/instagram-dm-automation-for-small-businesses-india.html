Why fast replies matter

Someone asking “price?” is ready to buy now. If they wait hours for an answer, many will have moved on. Automated replies make sure everyone gets the key information and a link immediately, even at night or when you're busy with customers.

List your most common questions

Scroll through your last 50 DMs and comments and note what people ask most. It's usually:

  • Price and offers
  • Sizes, menu or services
  • Location and timings
  • Booking or ordering
  • Delivery, COD and payment options

Each of these becomes a keyword with its own reply.

Ready-to-use replies by business

Clothing and online stores

  • PRICE → “Here's the price and all colours 👇” + product link
  • SIZE → “Here's our size chart. Still unsure? Reply with your height and usual size.”
  • COD → “Yes, COD is available across India. Here's how to order:”

Salons and spas

  • BOOK → “Book your slot here 💇” + booking link
  • RATES → “Here's our service menu with prices:”

Cafés and restaurants

  • MENU → “Here's today's menu 🍽️” + menu link
  • ORDER → “Order online here, or call us:”

Coaches and tutors

  • DEMO → “Book your free demo class here:”
  • FEES → “Here are the course details and fees:”

Set these up with DM keyword replies, and use comment to DM on product posts so “price?” comments get the same answer.

Tips for replies that convert

  • Lead with the answer. Price first, details after.
  • One button, one action: order, book or view.
  • Sound like you. Hinglish is fine if that's how you talk to customers.
  • Offer a human. “Reply here and our team will help” keeps trust high.
  • Thank people who tag you with story mention replies; a small discount brings them back.
