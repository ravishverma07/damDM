How to switch

  1. Open the Instagram app and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰), then Settings and activity.
  3. Tap Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account.
  4. Choose a category that describes you, then choose Creator or Business.
  5. Follow the remaining steps. You can skip the optional ones.

Instagram changes its menus from time to time, so the names may differ slightly on your phone.

Allow access to messages

For automated DMs, one more setting must be on:

  1. Go to Settings and activity → Messages and story replies → Message controls.
  2. Under Connected tools, turn on Allow access to messages.

Common questions

Will switching change my followers or posts?

No. Your followers, posts and username stay the same.

Can a private account be professional?

No. Professional accounts are public.
