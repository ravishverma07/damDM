Before you start

  • {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="check" size=16 stroke="2.4" %}An Instagram Business or Creator account. Not sure? See how to switch.
  • {% include "partials/icon.html" with name="check" size=16 stroke="2.4" %}In the Instagram app, go to Settings → Messages and story replies → Message controls → Connected tools, and turn on Allow access to messages.

1. Create your account and connect Instagram

Sign up with email or Google, then choose Connect Instagram. You'll log in on Instagram's own page and approve access. {{ APP_NAME }} never sees your password.

2. Create your first automation

Go to Automations → New and pick a template:

  • Send a link when people comment
  • Grow followers before sharing
  • Auto-reply to a DM keyword
  • Reply when people answer your story
  • Thank people who mention you

Choose the posts, set your keywords, write your message and button, then turn it on.

3. Start your free trial

Automations run while you have an active trial or plan. Start the 14-day trial from Plan & billing; see pricing for details.

4. Test it

Ask a friend to comment your keyword or send it by DM. Always test from a different Instagram account, the way your followers will use it.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happens when someone comments

  • Check the automation is on and your trial or plan is active.
  • Check the keyword matches what they typed, and the automation covers that post.
  • Make sure Allow access to messages is on in Instagram.

My Instagram account shows as disconnected

Reconnect it from the Instagram page in {{ APP_NAME }}. This can happen after a password change or if access was removed in Instagram.

Still stuck?

Email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} with your Instagram username and what you tried. You can also read the FAQ.
